About

Last updated: October 9, 2026 · App: LearnGuage

What LearnGuage is

LearnGuage is an Android app for learning a language with real content. You read graded books and watch videos, tap any word you don't know to see its translation, and save it. Saved words go into a spaced-repetition system, alongside vocabulary packs and grammar topics.

You can learn English, Spanish, German and French. The app interface is available in English, Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese, French and German.

Who builds it

LearnGuage is founded, built and run by Sinan Gümüşkabak, an independent developer. It is a self-funded project with no outside investors.

On Google Play the app is published under the developer name educationary studios (package name com.sinandede.learnguage).
