Account & Data Deletion

App: LearnGuage · Privacy Policy

1. Delete your account inside the app (recommended)

You can delete your account and all associated data yourself, instantly, from within the app:

  1. Open LearnGuage and sign in
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Under the Account section, tap Delete Account
  4. Type the confirmation word shown on screen and confirm

Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

2. Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app (lost device, forgotten password, uninstalled app), send a deletion request to sinan@learnguage.app from the email address registered to your LearnGuage account, with the subject "Account deletion request". Your account and data will be deleted within 30 days at the latest, and you will receive a confirmation reply.

3. What gets deleted

  • Profile information (display name, email link, avatar)
  • All learning data (saved words and sentences, progress, language-pair data, daily study records, statistics)
  • Images you attached to saved words (removed from file storage)
  • Subscription record held by the app (note: to stop billing, also cancel the subscription in Google Play → Subscriptions — deleting the account does not cancel a Google Play subscription)
  • The authentication account itself

Deletion is permanent and irreversible. No additional copies of your personal data are retained after deletion. Books/PDFs you added yourself are stored only on your device and are removed when the app's data is cleared.
