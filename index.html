Learn a language with real content

Read books, watch videos, tap any word you don't know, see its translation instantly and save it. Every saved word enters a smart spaced-repetition system — it comes back right before you'd forget it.

Get it on Google Play

Free to use. Premium removes the daily limits.

What you can do

📖 Learn by reading

  • Books matched to your level (A1–C1 in English; A1–A2 in Spanish, German and French)
  • Tap an unknown word for an instant translation
  • Long-press a sentence to see its translation and add it to your grammar practice
  • Add your own books and study with them

🎧 Learn by listening

  • Get used to real speech with subtitled videos
  • Synced transcript: the current line is highlighted as it's spoken
  • Tap any word in the transcript to translate and save it
  • Adjust playback speed to your pace

🧠 Memorize vocabulary

  • Spaced repetition (SRS): words return exactly when you need them
  • Learn Mode: multiple choice, writing, fill-in-the-blank
  • Flashcards, matching and word-ordering games
  • Attach images to your words to remember them better

✏️ Practice grammar

  • Save sentences from what you read and watch
  • Reinforce them with sentence-ordering, quizzes and matching
  • Build your own grammar set from the sentences you save

Free and Premium

LearnGuage is free to use. Free accounts get a daily allowance that is spent while unlocking book sections and watching listening content.

  • Premium removes the daily limits on reading and listening
  • Full access to every book and video in the catalogue
  • The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial
  • Cancel any time in Google Play → Subscriptions

Prices are shown inside the app in your local currency, and the price displayed at the time of purchase applies.

Frequently asked questions

Is LearnGuage free?
Yes. You can read, listen, and study vocabulary for free within a daily allowance. Premium removes those daily limits.
Which languages can I learn?
English, Spanish, German and French. The app interface is available in English, Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese, French and German.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Open Google Play → Subscriptions and cancel there. You keep Premium until the end of the period you already paid for.
How do I delete my account?
In the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. If you can't access the app, see the account deletion page.
Does the app record audio or track my location?
No. LearnGuage does not access your microphone or location. The app does show optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob — watching one is never required to use any part of the app, and Premium removes ads entirely. See the privacy policy.
Can I study with my own books?
Yes. You can add your own books and use the same tap-to-translate and word-saving tools with them.
